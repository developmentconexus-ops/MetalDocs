## Architecture notes (surface to hub, out of unit-3.2 boundary)
- **Fixed role enums are pre-existing, not introduced here.** ActorSelector storage
  (`approval_route_stage_selectors.role` = free text, no value CHECK) and the resolver
  (`WHERE role = $role`, any string) are role-agnostic — a future tenant-defined role
