## 2. Authority and invariants tested

The packet tested these core properties:

1. every business fact has one authority;
2. provider/editor/repository technology never becomes business identity;
3. Approval/Rendition/Release bind the same exact immutable `RevisionSubmission` / digest;
4. `DRAFT` is mutable persisted working truth and `SUBMITTED` is the immutable review boundary;
5. no confirmed Artifact exists without a governed semantic owner;
6. Dossier is documentary context, not ERP/PLM/custom-object authority;
7. retention expiry is eligibility, never automatic deletion;
8. active LegalHold prevents disposal of every governed retention subject within its live scope;
9. imported historical uncertainty remains uncertainty;
10. projections/jobs/providers may fail without rewriting canonical business truth.

## 3. Promoted findings

### F1 — Submission coherence — PROMOTED

A `RevisionSubmission` freezes one coherent governed state. Primary Artifact, governed metadata and decision-relevant structured/template provenance correspond to one accepted WorkingContent version and cannot be independently snapshotted across concurrent DRAFT changes.

### F2 — WorkingContent is format-agnostic — PROMOTED

`WorkingContent` is mutable persisted DRAFT authority independent of editor/provider/format:

```text
WorkingContent
= current primary Artifact
+ governed metadata
+ optional structured authoring state
+ working_version
```

EigenPal is one DOCX authoring provider around this state.

### F3 — external DRAFT replacement semantics — PROMOTED / bounded reopen closed

The former blanket rule “external edit fails on stale base” was replaced.

While a Revision remains DRAFT, an authorized actor may deliberately replace current WorkingContent. All DRAFT mutations participate in one OCC/`working_version`; OCC prevents overlapping-operation races, not deliberate replacement after observing current state. MetalDocs does not track/infer arbitrary offline-file ancestry and does not require long checkout.

Independent review B3 added the promoted enforcement clarification: replacement is whole-WorkingContent. Replacing primary Artifact also dispositions representation-dependent structured authoring state/provenance in the same OCC step while preserving immutable historical/template-seeding provenance.

### F4 — exact-Submission review surface — PROMOTED

Approval review resolves only the exact Submission being decided. Editors/viewers/previews/renditions are representations, never authority. Unsupported/unproven inline review uses a supported inspection path for that same Submission instead of creating a second canonical artifact.

### F5 — prospective LegalHold materialization — PROMOTED

Active Document/Dossier holds materialize current and newly entering retention subjects while they are within the live hold scope; unlink/lifecycle cannot release already-materialized held subjects. Dossier hold is documentary-context scope, not a generic custodian/ESI graph.

Independent review B1 confirmed that genuinely post-scope future subjects are outside the Dossier hold unless separately held. B2 confirmed never-submitted DRAFT/ESI preservation remains an explicit future trigger.

### F6 — contextual links never grant — PROMOTED

Dossier/Document/Evidence links are context only. Cross-scope operations authorize each governed target against its canonical scope/relationship. Search/timeline/export projections reapply canonical AuthZ.

### F7 — export completeness — PROMOTED

Export completeness is explicit and authorization-safe. A contract claiming a complete package fails closed rather than silently omitting required subjects the actor cannot read.
