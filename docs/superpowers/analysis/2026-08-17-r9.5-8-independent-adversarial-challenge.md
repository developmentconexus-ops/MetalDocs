## B. Material findings

**None at blocker severity.** Three residual observations survived attack; none invalidated a locked decision.

### B1 — LOW / OBSERVATION — hold-scope boundary semantics

An actor with `dossier.manage` may unlink a Document from a held Dossier; retention subjects created after genuine scope exit do not enter that Dossier hold.

Disposition: **ACCEPTED / NO CHANGE.** The Dossier hold protects subjects while inside its live documentary-context scope and preserves already-materialized held subjects after unlink. A direct Document/Evidence LegalHold is the existing seam for broader preservation. Dossier hold is not a generic custodian/ESI graph.

### B2 — LOW / DECLARED DEFERRAL — never-submitted DRAFT

Never-submitted DRAFT content remains outside LegalHold record coverage.

Disposition: **ACCEPTED / DECLARED DEFERRAL.** Full eDiscovery/ESI preservation is an explicit future trigger, not a hidden V1 requirement.

### B3 — LOW / WORDING — WorkingContent replacement holism

If replacement were implemented as only a primary-Artifact pointer swap, stale structured-authoring state could survive beside new bytes.

Disposition: **APPLIED.** The promoted R9.5-3 authority now states that replacement is whole-WorkingContent: replacing primary Artifact must disposition representation-dependent structured state/provenance in the same OCC step, while immutable historical/template-seeding provenance remains preserved.
